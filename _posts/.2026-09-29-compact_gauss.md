The paper highlights that the number of priors that common place 3DGS methods initialize/use in order to reconstruct a scene is randomly chosen even despite the fact that the contribution they have to the final scene fidelity and quaity is significant.

And so we will go into the details later but the rough idea is that the authors derive the optimal number of gaussians you need for a scene by looking at the "surface area" of sorts available before training and the actual "content"of the scene is known during thre training period and we can use these two in some way to pick the righ numberr of gaussians that need duplication/removal. 

Constraints that we have:
- Blender must run completely headless.
- The transforms must be known.
- The horse placement must be accurate and consistent across scenes.
