Q. 1 - Is there camera involved in the Parametric model sampling that is done?

The zoom added (scale being multiplied a samples floating point number between 0.99 and 1.0 is just for zooming in or zooming out)

In the pose sampling from Pferd - remember that are only sampling offsets for joints - not each vertex and these joints offsets are being derived from real world horses. How you ask?

There are thousands of poses present from real horses in pferd - captured from mocap markers - and we sample one at a time randomly - which gives us 6 numbers because these are 6D rotation representations. And the way to obtain the 3D rotation vector from these is that we take the two columns, normalize the first one to get the first axis, orthonormalize the second one to get the second axis perpendicular to the first one and then take the cross product of these two to obtain the third axes.

So you have the 3 x 3 rotation matrix which is then handed over to scipy to get the axis angle representation - i.e the horse rotates by how much radians about which axis. 

So overall:
The shape is betas for 37 basis vectors that decides how much will each joint stretch or squish in that particular direction.
The pose is the per joint axis angles - 3 numbers encoding around which axis is the horse mesh rotating and so on.
The global orient is the axis angle representation for the entire horse - where is it oriented, is it facing left/facing right or something else?

Also, when sampling the VAREN params - the original mesh just exists with the origin being the centre of the mesh. And all the tx, ty and the params that we see being derived in weak perspective etc. are just there to nicely project and centre the image onto the 2d Canvas that will become the RGB image.

### How does the weak perspective assumption helps us:


- Perspective distortion is how much apparent size changes with depth under a real pinhole camera?
- So having a large focal length has the advantage that you are looking at the horse from far away which then leads to the assumption that the entire image is at a consistent depth. Which allows us to not deal with per-pixel depth anymore. And this is a direct inspiration from the Animer+ train which uses weak perspective and simplify the calculation.
