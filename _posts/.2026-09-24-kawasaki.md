The imitation learning for learning motion from horse mocap for our Mujoco Model that we need to do. Here we have mocap data and we need to transfer the motion data from the mocap captures to the horse that we have designed in Mujoco. And before we start solving this problem for our horse, let us try to understand a few works that have been done in this space previously:

- DeepMimic: The central idea here is that pipelines that rely on imitation learning / replaying captured data can be pretty accurate and natural looking, but they are highliy likely to fail when the character tries to imitate a motion not in the space of observed motion sequences. Similarily methods that rely on accurate physics interpretation, collisions and forces "react" to new scenarios pretty well but designing such rigorous physics informed controllers is difficult for every task. So deep mimic combines this idea of learning from Ground Truth trajectory, but also using RL to adapt to OOD data that is not present in the input trajectories or recorded data.

So one can think of DeepMimic as being something similar to StyleTransfer experiments where the idea is not just making policies and networks achieve results but achieve results in a particular way.
