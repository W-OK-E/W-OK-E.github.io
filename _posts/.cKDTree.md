A KD Tree is a way to organize points in space so that nearest neighbor scans are close, and this is done by splitting the points in x,y,z. 
The steps can be thought of as:
1. Take the set of all the points and then subdivide the entire volume into multiple sub-volumes and then when you need to look for points that are closest to a given point, you will only look for points in that sub-volume and not the entire set of points - which makes lookup much easier and faster.

So in the simplest terms the way k-d tree works are as follows:


You have all the points in the mesh - You organize the points into a tree and the split at every level is on the basis of an axes (which coordinate - x or y is bigger and then you split the node). Once every point is organized, we bring a query point and then traverse the tree from the root node to the leaf comparing every node. The crucial part here is that - when comparing the Query point's coordinate with the target coordinate at a particular level - we will check the coordinate on the basis of whose axes the level was split and then work with that.

We calculate the distance between the Query point and one point at each level and then remove all the other points at that level which do not fall under the same distance threshold.
