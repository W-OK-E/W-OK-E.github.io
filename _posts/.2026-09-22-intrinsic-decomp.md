So we are trying to do this task:

Generate a mesh -> Wrap a texture onto it -> Position the mesh in a Unreal Engine Level or scene and capture snapshots along with the relevant camera metadata.

But there is an issue here - The textures that we are wrapping onto the mesh are generated via diffusion models and hence have lighting pre-baked into them. Which means when rendering snapshots in Unreal Engine or any other Animation Engine for that matter, there is the lighting from the environment alongside the lighting that is pre-baked into the texture which causes all sorts of problems. And we need to get rid of the pre-baked lighting that the diffusion generated texture comes with.

So we will be talking about this paper called "IDArb" published in ICCV 2024 that talks about intrinsic decomposition and does what we want it to.
